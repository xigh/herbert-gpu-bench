Here are my notes on a hybrid language model, repeated as I took them during several meetings.

Meeting 1: The GatedDeltaNet layer keeps a recurrent state per value head instead of a key-value cache. At every position the state decays by a learned factor, the key retrieves what the state predicts for it, and the difference between the value and that prediction, scaled by a learned rate, is written back as a rank-one update. The query then reads the updated state. Three such layers alternate with one full attention layer, which keeps a classic cache of keys and values and attends over every earlier position with a partial rotary embedding on a quarter of each head.
